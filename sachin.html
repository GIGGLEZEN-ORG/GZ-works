Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding part fitment, delivery times across India, authenticity guarantees, warranty, and returns.

How do I ensure a spare part matches my exact car model and variant?

Use our Choose Your Car selector at the top of the home page to specify your car brand, model, and manufacturing year. All catalog parts remain visible and can be added regardless of the selected car. If your car has a specific engine trim, facelift variant, or aftermarket modification, our automotive technicians can cross-check your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) or registration details over WhatsApp or phone call to help confirm exact fitment.

Are your automobile spare parts 100% genuine and brand new?

Yes, 100%. Every spare part available on Sachin Automobiles HUB is brand new and sourced directly from certified Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM) and tier-1 Original Equipment Suppliers (OES). All products come sealed in authentic packaging with tamper-evident holograms, manufacturer batch codes, and official tax invoices.

What is the delivery timeline across India, and can I track my shipment?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are processed and dispatched the same day via premier courier partners (Bluedart, Delhivery, DTDC). Major metro cities receive delivery within 24 to 48 hours, while other regions are serviced in 2 to 4 business days. You receive live SMS and WhatsApp tracking links at every milestone of your parcel's journey.

What is your Return, Exchanges and Refund Policy?

We offer a generous 10-day hassle-free return and exchange policy from the date of delivery. If an item doesn't fit or arrives with shipping damage, you can request a pickup through our Customer Support desk. Once the item is collected by our courier and inspected, your replacement is dispatched immediately or a 100% refund is issued to your original payment method within 24–48 hours.

What warranty is provided with the car spare parts?

All functional mechanical and electrical components (including brake discs, alternators, radiators, batteries, and sensors) carry standard manufacturer warranties ranging from 6 months up to 2 years covering manufacturing defects. Your official GST purchase invoice acts as valid proof of warranty nationwide.

Which payment methods are accepted, and is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?

Yes! Cash on Delivery (COD) is available across all serviceable pin codes in India. We also accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 50+ banks, and all major Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay). Every online transaction is fortified with 256-bit bank-grade SSL encryption.

Can I visit your physical branch or pick up parts directly in Hyderabad?

Yes! You are warmly welcome to visit our main branch located in Lingampally, Hyderabad, Telangana. Our in-house technical team is available Monday through Saturday (9:00 AM – 8:30 PM IST) for on-the-spot fitment consultations, physical part inspections, and direct counter purchases.
