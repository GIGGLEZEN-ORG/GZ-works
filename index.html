Crafted for the Discerning Connoisseur of Slow Luxury

Nestled between whispered botanical courtyards and the vibrant cultural pulse of the city, Aurelia was envisioned not merely as a hotel, but as a residential private sanctuary where architectural purity meets intuitive warmth.

Each of our forty-two suites is individually curated with hand-honed travertine, bespoke white oak furnishings, rare vintage acoustics, and museum-grade contemporary art. Here, unhurried time becomes the ultimate luxury.

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Everything you need to know about your arrival, private accommodations, and hotel policies.

Check-in commences at 15:00, and check-out is extended until 12:00 noon. Guests arriving earlier or wishing to depart later are cordially invited to relax in our Spa Sanctuary or Private Library with luggage securely attended by the concierge.
Complimentary chauffeur transfer in our electric luxury fleet is included for all guests residing in the Grand Aurelia Penthouse and Presidential Sanctuary Suite. For all other suites, private airport chauffeur service may be seamlessly arranged during booking for a preferred flat fee.
Standard suite reservations may be modified or cancelled without penalty up to 72 hours prior to arrival date. Advance purchase promotional rates and specialty holiday packages may carry dedicated terms clearly outlined at reservation time.
