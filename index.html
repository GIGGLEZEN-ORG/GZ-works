About Our Company

Our story is rooted in thoughtful luxury.

Lumina Resort & Spa was created to bring contemporary European elegance, warm hospitality, and restorative wellbeing into one unforgettable coastal destination.

Founded by a family of hospitality visionaries, Lumina began with a simple ambition: to create a place where exceptional service, cultural authenticity, and natural beauty could live in perfect harmony.

From our first private villa retreat to the full-scale sanctuary you see today, every decision has been guided by calm, craftsmanship, and care. We believe luxury should feel personal, immersive, and deeply restorative.
